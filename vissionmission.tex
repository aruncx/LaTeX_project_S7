	\section*{\centering Institution Vision}
	%
	
	To be a premier institution in engineering education and research, nurturing innovative professionals for sustainable societal development. 
	
	\section*{\centering Institution Mission }
	
	\textbf{M1:} Provide quality engineering education that empowers students with strong technical competence and analytical problem solving abilities. 
	
	\noindent \textbf{M2:} Promote research, innovation, entrepreneurship and industry collaboration to develop sustainable technological solutions. 
	
	\noindent \textbf{M3:} Develop ethical and socially responsible professionals with leadership qualities, lifelong learning abilities and a commitment to societal well-being. 
	
	
	
	\section*{\centering Department Vision}
	To be a reputed student centric department in electrical and electronics engineering, fostering innovative and
	ethically driven engineering professionals dedicated to sustainable societal development.
	
	\section*{\centering Department Mission}
	
	\noindent \textbf{M1:} Impart quality education in electrical and electronics engineering that equips students with strong theoretical knowledge, practical engineering skills, and problem-solving ability.
	
	\noindent \textbf{M2:} Nurture a culture of research, innovation, and entrepreneurship in electrical and electronics Engineering for sustainable technological solutions.
	
	\noindent \textbf{M3:} Develop professionally competent graduates with ethical integrity, leadership qualities, and social responsibility for lifelong learning and societal service.
	
	\section*{\centering Department PEOs}
	
	\noindent \textbf{PEO-1:} Graduates will apply strong foundational knowledge of mathematics, engineering sciences, and electrical and electronics engineering to formulate, analyze, and solve real-life problems in circuits, systems, and industrial
	installations.
	
	\noindent \textbf{PEO-2:} Graduates will pursue research, innovation, and entrepreneurship, engaging in lifelong learning and higher studies to create sustainable and impactful technological advancements.
	
	\noindent \textbf{PEO-3:} Graduates will demonstrate ethical integrity, leadership, effective communication, and multidisciplinary teamwork, contributing responsibly to societal and technological progress.
	
	\section*{\centering Department PSOs}
	
	\noindent \textbf{PSO-1:} Apply the fundamentals of electrical and electronics engineering, circuit theory, control, and instrumentation to
	analyze, design, and develop efficient electrical systems and solutions.
	
	\noindent \textbf{PSO-2:} Integrate and apply knowledge of power systems, renewable energy, power electronics, electrical machines, drives, and electric vehicles to develop sustainable, economical, and industry-relevant solutions to electrical engineering
	problems.
	
	\noindent \textbf{PSO-3:} Engage in continuous learning and apply modern engineering tools and advanced technologies to address emerging challenges in electrical and electronics engineering.
	
	\section*{\centering Department POs}
	
	\textbf{Engineering Graduates will be able to:}
	
	\noindent \textbf{PO1: Engineering Knowledge:} Apply knowledge of mathematics, natural science, computing, engineering fundamentals and an engineering specialization as specified in WK1 to WK4
	respectively to develop to the solution of complex engineering problems.
	
	\noindent \textbf{PO2: Problem Analysis:} Identify, formulate, review research literature and analyze complex 	engineering problems reaching substantiated conclusions with consideration for
	sustainable development. (WK1 to WK4)
	
	\noindent \textbf{PO3: Design/Development of Solutions:} Design creative solutions for complex engineering problems and design/ develop systems/components/processes to meet identified needs with consideration for the public health and safety, whole-life cost, net zero carbon, culture, society and environment as required. (WK5)
	
	\noindent \textbf{PO4: Conduct Investigations of Complex Problems:} Conduct investigations of complex 	engineering problems using research-based knowledge including design of experiments, modelling, analysis \& interpretation of data to provide valid conclusions. (WK8).
	
	\noindent \textbf{PO5: Engineering Tool Usage:} Create, select and apply appropriate techniques, resources and modern engineering \& IT tools, including prediction and modelling recognizing their limitations to solve complex engineering problems. (WK2 and WK6)
	
	\noindent \textbf{PO6: The Engineer and The World:} Analyze and evaluate societal and environmental aspects
	while solving complex engineering problems for its impact on sustainability with reference to economy, health, safety, legal framework, culture and environment. (WK1,
	WK5, and WK7).
	
	\noindent \textbf{PO7: Ethics:} Apply ethical principles and commit to professional ethics, human values,
	diversity and inclusion; adhere to national \& international laws. (WK9)
	
	\noindent \textbf{PO8: Individual and Collaborative Team work:} Function effectively as an individual, and as a member or leader in diverse/multi-disciplinary teams.
	
	\noindent \textbf{PO9: Communication:} Communicate effectively and inclusively within the engineering community and society at large, such as being able to comprehend and write effective reports and design documentation, make effective presentations considering cultural, language, and learning differences
	
	\noindent \textbf{PO10: Project Management and Finance:} Apply knowledge and understanding of engineering
	management principles and economic decision-making and apply these to one’s own work, as a member and leader in a team, and to manage projects and in multidisciplinary
	environments.
	
	\noindent \textbf{PO11: Life-Long Learning:} Recognize the need for, and have the preparation and ability for i) independent and life-long learning ii) adaptability to new and emerging technologies and iii) critical thinking in the broadest context of technological change. (WK8)
